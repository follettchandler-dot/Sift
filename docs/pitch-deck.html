The Endgame

The card that knows
what you bought

Every card in existence shows where you shopped. The Sift Card shows what you bought. Swipe at a gas station — see "$32 fuel, $9 snacks, $6 energy drinks" in 3 seconds. No scanning. No emails. Just swipe.

Why this works

As the card issuer, we receive Level 3 item data directly from the merchant's payment processor. No partnerships needed. No manual effort. The data flows to us by default.

Why nobody’s done it

Banks profit from ignorance — more spending = more fees. Ramp and Brex issue cards but only show merchant-level data. No card has ever shown item-level data. We’d be the first.

Why Now

Five tailwinds converging

01

AI accuracy crossed the threshold

94% categorization accuracy was impossible 2 years ago. Modern LLMs make item extraction and classification reliable enough for production use.

02

Digital receipts are standard

80%+ of major retailers now email receipts. Five years ago it was 30%. The data is finally accessible at scale.

03

BaaS makes card issuing possible

Marqeta, Lithic, Unit — you can now launch a card program without a banking license. This was impossible for startups 5 years ago.

04

Consumer data rights are expanding

CFPB Section 1033 established that consumers own their financial data. Regulatory momentum is on the side of data portability.

05

Alternative data market exploding

$7.3B market growing 40% CAGR. Hedge funds and CPG brands are desperate for item-level purchase data. Nobody has it at scale.
